LESS SEARCHING, MORE FISHING

Local fishing information, all in one place

Use tide charts and tables to plan around predicted highs and lows, check moon phases, review river conditions, and compare wind and weather before choosing a fishing spot. The Bay page includes species-specific status indicators and explanations when available, with red, green, or yellow circles to make closures, open categories, and items needing verification easier to scan. Browse the Species guide for local fish and crabs, read fishing reports and community stories, and use the site navigation to move between tools. Data availability and regulations can change; status indicators are informational, may rely on cached data if an official source cannot be reached, and do not replace checking current official regulations, emergency closures, health advisories, and local restrictions before fishing or keeping a catch.
